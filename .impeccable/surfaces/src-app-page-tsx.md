# Home

mode: experience

## Audience

Someone opening Adrian's site to see who he is and whether the public work is relevant. One visit, desktop or a 390px phone.

## Job

Read the role, scan three real repositories, and leave through a real link.

## Constraints

Bentolio bento is binding: top bar, headline with italic and bold, line flower, portrait, project rail with a preview on the first item, bio, accent contact card, social strip. Do not copy the template's photos, names, or sentences. Accent is not the template salmon. No invented metrics, clients, testimonials, or quotes. LinkedIn and email stay TODO. No analytics.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The first screen is a single bento of Adrian's public facts, refusing both the case-study essay and a metric hero.
OWN-WORLD: Warm near-white ground, blush rounded cards, near-black ink, Urbanist, one deep cobalt contact card that replaces the template salmon. A line-drawn bloom sits in the headline card; the portrait is an authored abstract plate until a photo exists.
STORY: The visitor reads the role, sees Barcelona housing and the two parking models, and can open GitHub. Contact is the cobalt card, linked only to a known URL.
FIRST VIEWPORT: At 1280px the bar spans the grid; below it three columns — headline, portrait, project rail — then bio and contact under the first two, socials closing the rail. The headline is the largest type. Contact is the primary action. At 390px the same regions stack in one column: bar, headline, portrait, projects, bio, contact, socials.
FORM: Brief-pinned Bentolio bento, adapted. Seed 04dca6ec assigned an unwritten candidate 4; the brief overrides that roll. Skate-wall, console, shader, six-pack, ribbon, and Miura challengers are declined because they would drop the pinned structure.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
